Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, pricing, and policies.

Do you provide hairpieces?

expand_more

Yes, I can purchase hairpieces for you. Please let me know when booking. Prices do not include hairpieces unless stated otherwise on the specific style.

Do you do house calls?

expand_more

Yes, I offer house calls in Durban (Berea area only). A transport fee applies. I do not take taxis inside Durban.

What is your cancellation policy?

expand_more

Please cancel early so I can add another client. I require confirmation the day before your appointment.

Do I need to wash my hair before coming?

expand_more

Yes, please wash and blow-dry your hair before coming to your appointment. This ensures the best results.

What areas do you cover?

expand_more

My studio is in Sandton (2 Sandown Valley Crescent). I also offer house calls in Durban, Berea area.

Do you do haircuts?

expand_more

No, I specialize only in braiding services for women and men. No haircuts or fades.

What payment methods do you accept?

expand_more

Cash and instant EFT are accepted. Please confirm payment details when booking.

Are weekends available?

expand_more

Saturday and Sunday are by appointment only. Please book in advance.
