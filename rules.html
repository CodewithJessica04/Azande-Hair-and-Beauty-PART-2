Salon Rules & Policies

Please review our policies before booking. These ensure a smooth experience for everyone.

Wash & Blow-Dry Required

Please wash and blow-dry your hair before coming to your appointment. This prepares your hair for braiding and ensures the best results.

confirmation_number

Confirm Your Booking

Please confirm your booking the day before your appointment. Unconfirmed bookings may be released to other clients.

cancel

Early Cancellation

If you wish to cancel, please cancel early so that I can add another client instead. Last-minute cancellations affect my ability to serve others.

monetization_on

Pricing Policy

Prices do not include hairpiece unless stated otherwise on the specific style. Please ask if you need me to purchase a hairpiece for you.

House Call Policy

I work around Durban, Berea for house calls. A transport fee applies. I do not take taxis inside Durban.

Late Arrival

Please arrive on time. Late arrivals may result in shortened appointment time or rescheduling to accommodate other clients.

Weekend Appointments

Saturday and Sunday are by appointment only. Walk-ins are welcome Monday through Friday at the Sandton studio.
