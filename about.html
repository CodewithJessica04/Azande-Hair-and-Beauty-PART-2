About Azande

Azande Madlala

Azande is a master braider with over 8 years of experience specializing in Lemonade braids, Cornrows, Free hand styles, and Pondo. Her journey began in Durban, where she built a loyal clientele before establishing her flagship studio in Sandton.

Known for precision, cleanliness, and artistic flair, Azande has grown her following to over 27,000 on Instagram, becoming one of South Africa's most sought-after braiding specialists.

Azande believes that every client deserves a luxurious experience — from the moment they book to the final reveal. She uses only premium products supplied by Darling, ensuring long-lasting, healthy braids.
